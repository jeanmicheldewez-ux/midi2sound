Browser MIDI Sound Engine

Standalone demo for reusing ultrasound.js outside the Ultragear ESP32 controller UI. It can be used as a reference for Midiboy, BOW, Dancing5, WATTOO, and hardware-free browser instruments.

Computer keys: A S D F G H J K play C D E F G A B C. W and X change octave.
